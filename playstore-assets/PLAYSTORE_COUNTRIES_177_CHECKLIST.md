# Global production availability

- In Play Console, open **Production > Countries/regions**.
- Select **All countries/regions (177)** and save the selection.
- Confirm that the production release uses `versionName 1.0.10` and `versionCode 138` before starting the 100% rollout.
- The app has no Play Billing subscriptions, so country-specific subscription prices do not apply.
- Country availability is a Play Console setting and is not stored inside the APK or AAB.

Localization follows the user's device/app language. `en-US` is the fallback when a country has no dedicated Play Store locale.
