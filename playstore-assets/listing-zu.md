## Full description
I-Visor Crypto iletha Bitcoin nezintengo ze-altcoin, izimpawu zobuchwepheshe, umlando wekholi, izindaba ze-crypto ezihlungiwe kanye nezinkomba ezinkulu kudeshibhodi eyodwa yemakethe esheshayo.

Qapha amathuba, hlola Fed Watch, landela ikhalenda lezomnotho lase-US futhi ubuyekeze umlando wesiginali ngemiphumela engu-1h, 2h kanye ne-4h.

Amasignali anolwazi futhi asekelwe kudatha yemakethe. Akuzona izeluleko zezimali.
